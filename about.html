Understand the data

Entries come from CISA’s Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalogue, loaded from its official GitHub JSON feed. Newest means most recently added to the catalogue, not most recently discovered.

“Unknown” ransomware use does not mean no ransomware use. Inclusion does not establish that your systems are affected or currently under attack. This feed does not include CVSS severity scores.

About IntelScope

IntelScope is a threat intelligence platform focused on vulnerabilities with evidence of exploitation. It brings CISA catalogue entries into a searchable view to help security teams identify relevant threats and review mitigation guidance.

Find what matters to your environment

Search by CVE, product or keyword, narrow results by vendor, and explore vulnerabilities associated with known ransomware campaigns. Each entry includes a description, its catalogue addition date, CISA’s recommended action and a link to the National Vulnerability Database.

Turn intelligence into informed action

Use IntelScope to support vulnerability triage and prioritisation. Match the information to your asset inventory, affected versions and exposure, then consult vendor guidance to determine the appropriate response.
